ABOUT MIH

Our Vision

MIH is not just a hackathon. It is a battleground of innovation where thinkers become builders, coders become creators, and ideas become working solutions.
For 24 hours, the brightest student innovators from across institutions come together to design, develop, and deploy technology that solves real problems.
No spectators. Only creators.
Do you have what it takes to install your mind into innovation?

What is MIH ?

MIH stands for Mind Installers Hackathon — IIMT’s flagship national-level 24-hour hackathon built on one belief:
> Ideas are powerful only when implemented.

Previous Versions


Over its previous editions, MIH has evolved into a platform known for:

  • High-quality problem statements and impactful themes
  • Participation from talented students across institutions
  • Real prototypes, deployable solutions, and startup ideas
  • Professional execution, strong mentorship, and credible evaluation


MIH is recognized not just for competition, but for "creation, collaboration, and innovation under pressure".
MIH 4.0 raises the bar — larger scale, stronger partnerships, tougher challenges, and greater rewards.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Who can participate in the hackathon? +

The hackathon is open to all students, professionals, and tech enthusiasts. You can participate individually or in teams of up to 4 members.

Is there a registration fee? +

No, participation in Make It Happen is completely free! We believe in making innovation accessible to everyone.

What should I bring to the event? +

Bring your laptop, chargers, student ID, and your creativity! We'll provide WiFi, meals, snacks, and plenty of energy drinks.

Will accommodation be provided? +

Yes, sleeping arrangements and rest areas will be available for participants who need them during the 48-hour hackathon.

What if I don't have a team? +

Don't worry! We'll have a team formation session at the beginning where you can meet other participants and form teams.
